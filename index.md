## About Me

<p style="font-size: 16px;">
I am a M.S. student at <a href="https://gsai.kaist.ac.kr/">KAIST AI</a>, advised by Prof. <a href="https://bispl.weebly.com/professor.html">Jong Chul Ye</a>.
I am currently a research intern at the <a href="https://vectorinstitute.ai/">Vector Institute</a>, working with Prof. <a href="https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~y328yu/">Yaoliang Yu</a> and Prof. <a href="https://www.ricardobaptista.com/">Ricardo Baptista</a>.
I earned my B.S. in Naval Architecture and Ocean Engineering and Artificial Intelligence (Double Major) from <a href="https://snu.ac.kr/">Seoul National University (SNU)</a> in Feb. 2025.
</p>

<p style="font-size: 16px;">
My research focuses on diffusion and flow models for learning and inference from imperfect observations.
I develop efficient methods for image and video inverse problems and study generative-model training from corrupted data when clean data are scarce or unavailable.
I am interested in extending this background to computational pathology and medical image analysis, with an emphasis on robustness, quantitative accuracy, and clinically meaningful evaluation.
</p>
